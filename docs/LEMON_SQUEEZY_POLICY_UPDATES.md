## Privacy: replace “Microsoft Store purchases and licensing”

### Distribution, payments and licence verification

HYPNIX is distributed through the Microsoft Store. Microsoft processes information
associated with your Microsoft account, downloads and Store services under the
[Microsoft Privacy Statement](https://privacy.microsoft.com/en-us/privacystatement).

Paid HYPNIX licences are purchased through Lemon Squeezy. When you choose to buy,
HYPNIX opens a Lemon Squeezy checkout in your browser. Lemon Squeezy processes your
payment and associated checkout information under its terms and
[Privacy Policy](https://www.lemonsqueezy.com/privacy). Mablook may access order and
customer information made available through the merchant dashboard to administer
purchases, provide support and handle refunds. HYPNIX does not collect your payment
card details, and Mablook does not receive full card details through the application.

When you activate, check or deactivate a licence, HYPNIX sends your licence key and,
where applicable, an activation instance identifier to Lemon Squeezy over HTTPS.
Activation also sends a randomly generated installation identifier. This identifier
does not contain your Windows username, computer name or hardware serial number.
As part of these network requests, Lemon Squeezy receives connection information,
such as your IP address. Its responses may include customer and order metadata;
HYPNIX does not retain customer names or email addresses from those responses.

Licence checks occur at startup and periodically while the application is running.
They are used to confirm validity and enforce activation limits. A confirmed
activation permits up to seven days of offline use, subject to the licence's own
expiration. Internet access is required to activate, deactivate and renew that
verification period.

HYPNIX stores the licence key, activation instance, random installation identifier,
trial start time and verification timestamps locally, protected by Windows for the
current user. These records support the 15-day trial, paid access and offline use.
They may remain after uninstalling. Removing local records does not automatically
deactivate an instance registered with Lemon Squeezy.

We process licensing and purchase information as necessary to provide the purchased
software, administer support and comply with applicable legal obligations. Relevant
service providers process information under their applicable privacy terms and
legal safeguards.

## Privacy: replace the broad no-transmission statement

Wallpaper content and audio analysis remain local and are not automatically uploaded
to Mablook. Licensing requests are sent to Lemon Squeezy as described above. Support
messages and attachments are transmitted only when you choose to send them.

## Terms: replace purchases and pricing

### Purchases and pricing

HYPNIX is available to install through the Microsoft Store. Installation does not
by itself grant a paid HYPNIX licence. Paid licences are sold through Lemon Squeezy
as a one-time purchase without a recurring subscription.

The checkout shows the applicable price, taxes, activation allowance and purchase
conditions before you confirm payment. Prices may vary by region or change over
time. Lemon Squeezy handles checkout and payment under its applicable terms.

### Free trial

HYPNIX includes a 15-day trial starting when the application first initialises its
licensing state on your Windows profile. No payment card is required to begin the
trial. It does not automatically convert into a paid purchase.

When the trial ends, wallpaper playback stops and a valid paid licence is required
to continue. Your wallpaper files, presets and settings are not deleted merely
because the trial expires.

### Activation and connectivity

Enter the licence key from your Lemon Squeezy receipt in HYPNIX to activate it.
The activation allowance is the one stated in your purchase. Separate Windows
profiles can require separate activations. Multiple monitors attached to the same
activated Windows profile do not require additional activations.

Internet access is required for activation and deactivation, and at least once
every seven days for licence verification. A confirmed activation may continue to
work offline for up to seven days, unless the licence expires sooner. Access may
pause when verification is overdue or a licence is invalid, disabled or expired.

You may release an activation using “Deactivate this PC” in HYPNIX. Uninstalling
alone does not release an activation. If your device is unavailable or you need
help managing activations, contact hello@mablook.com. Keep your licence key private.

### Refunds and consumer rights

For billing or refund assistance, use the options in your Lemon Squeezy receipt or
contact hello@mablook.com. Refund requests are handled through the applicable
Lemon Squeezy procedures and consumer law. A refunded or otherwise invalidated
licence may no longer permit paid access.

Nothing in these terms limits mandatory rights relating to defective or
non-conforming digital content, refunds or withdrawal. Where withdrawal rights may
end upon the supply of digital content, this applies only when the legally required
consent and acknowledgement have been obtained.
