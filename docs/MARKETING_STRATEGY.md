# HYPNIX positioning and sales strategy

Product-owner direction recorded on 2026-09-21. This is the reference for website,
Microsoft Store, checkout and campaign copy. Public-channel updates are tracked in
[release pending items](RELEASE_PENDING.md); this document does not publish them.

## Core positioning

**Relax. Have fun. Be cool.**

HYPNIX brings atmosphere, music and personal style to the Windows desktop. Put on
music in your usual player, watch audio-reactive animations respond in real time,
and make your desktop a place you enjoy spending time. Relaxation, fun and a great
looking setup are the reasons to buy; animated wallpapers are how HYPNIX delivers
that experience.

The product should feel expressive and easy to enjoy. A quiet visual break and a
more energetic music session both belong in the HYPNIX story. The user chooses the
mood through the wallpaper and its available controls.

## Three reasons to buy

| Pillar | Customer benefit | What to show |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Relax | Create a pleasant atmosphere for a break or a quiet moment. | A continuous, gently moving scene with restrained settings. |
| Have fun | Enjoy seeing your music come alive on the desktop. | A real recording of an audio-reactive scene responding to music. |
| Be cool | Give your desktop a look that feels like you. | Color, glow and composition changes on supported effects; different looks on each display. |

Lead sales messages with the experience, then demonstrate it, then explain the
features that make it possible. Audio reaction belongs near the beginning of a
demo or description, alongside atmosphere and personal style.

## Demonstration and channel plan

- **Website:** open with the brand line, a real desktop animation and the music
  reaction. Follow with the three benefits, customization and a clear trial CTA.
- **Microsoft Store:** lead the description with atmosphere, music and style;
  use real product screenshots and explain the trial and purchase terms clearly.
- **Lemon Squeezy:** carry the same positioning into the product description, then
  state the confirmed price, device allowance and activation instructions.
- **Short videos/social posts:** show a quiet scene, start music and show its live
  response, then change the look. End on “Relax. Have fun. Be cool.” and one CTA.
  Use music cleared for promotional use.

Suggested first demo: 20–30 seconds of the actual app and desktop. Show a calm
moment, a clearly visible response to music, and one customization change. Keep
the capture continuous enough to demonstrate real-time behavior. The existing
[screenshots](product-screenshots/README.md) illustrate the UI; a new recording is
still needed to demonstrate motion and audio reaction.

## Product truth in sales copy

- Music plays in the user's usual player; HYPNIX reacts to system playback. There
  is no bundled music catalog or built-in music player to advertise.
- Demonstrate audio reaction using a wallpaper that supports it and with
  **Audio reactive** enabled. Wallpaper and control support varies by renderer.
- Describe relaxation as an intended experience and atmosphere. Do not turn the
  animation-design notes into promises of medical or therapeutic effects.
- Use the [README](../README.md) and current validation reports for shipped
  capabilities. Ideas in [product direction](PRODUCT_DIRECTION.md), including
  playlists and web interactivity, are not automatically available features.
- Download and installation are free. The app includes a 15-day trial; continued
  use requires an external one-time licence purchase. The Live product confirmed
  on 2026-09-23 costs EUR 3.99 and allows five activations without licence expiry.
  Keep the free download separate from the paid licence in customer-facing copy.
  Follow [the commerce checklist](LEMON_SQUEEZY.md) before publishing offer details.

Operational ownership: Lemon Squeezy manages purchases and the commercial
operation. HYPNIX owns the application experience and its licence integration.
Sales materials should direct customers to the hosted checkout; they do not create
a requirement for us to build or validate payment, receipt or refund services.
See [responsibilities](LEMON_SQUEEZY.md#responsibility-boundary--owner-direction-2026-09-21).

Calm-scene authoring follows [relaxing animation design](RELAXING_ANIMATION_DESIGN.md).
It supports the Relax pillar; the broader product positioning also includes music,
fun and personal style.
