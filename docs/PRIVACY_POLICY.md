HYPNIX Privacy Policy
Last updated: 21 September 2026

Mablook publishes HYPNIX. For privacy questions or requests concerning information handled by Mablook, contact hello@mablook.com.

Local desktop and audio processing
HYPNIX uses display information, wallpaper preferences, selected local media and desktop window state to render wallpapers and apply automatic pause rules. Settings can include monitor identifiers and layout, local media paths, presets and playback preferences. These are stored locally in your Windows user profile.

When Audio reactive is enabled, HYPNIX analyzes system playback locally using Windows audio loopback to animate visuals. It does not open the microphone, record raw audio to files or upload audio or wallpaper content to Mablook. You can disable Audio reactive or stop wallpaper playback in the app. HYPNIX does not include advertising trackers or automatic analytics uploads.

Local diagnostics
HYPNIX keeps rotating diagnostic logs in its local application-data folder. Logs can contain timestamps, application and operating-system information, file paths and technical errors. Logs are not automatically sent to Mablook. Review and remove sensitive details before choosing to send a log or screenshot for support.

Licensing
HYPNIX includes a 15-day local trial. Paid activation, validation and deactivation communicate with Lemon Squeezy over HTTPS. Requests send your licence key and, when applicable, an activation instance identifier. Activation uses a randomly generated installation identifier, not your Windows username, computer name or hardware serial number. Lemon Squeezy also receives connection information such as your IP address.

The app uses the provider's response to verify licence status, the relevant product and activation limits. It does not retain customer names or email addresses from licence responses. Licence checks occur at startup and periodically during use. Confirmed access can continue offline for up to seven days, subject to licence expiration.

The licence key, activation instance, installation identifier and trial/verification timestamps are stored locally with Windows encryption for the current user. These records are used to administer the trial and paid access. They can remain after uninstalling. Use "Deactivate this PC" to release an activation; uninstalling or deleting local files does not itself release it at Lemon Squeezy.

Purchases and third-party services
When you choose to buy a licence, HYPNIX opens Lemon Squeezy checkout in your browser. Lemon Squeezy manages checkout, payment processing, receipts, taxes and billing/refund operations. HYPNIX does not collect payment card details. Mablook may use order or licence information made available by Lemon Squeezy to resolve activation and support requests.
Lemon Squeezy privacy policy: https://www.lemonsqueezy.com/privacy

Microsoft handles Microsoft Store accounts, downloads and Store services under its own privacy statement: https://privacy.microsoft.com/en-us/privacystatement
These providers may process information in other countries under their respective policies. Their websites and services have their own data practices.

Support, purpose and retention
If you contact hello@mablook.com, Mablook receives the contact details, message and attachments you choose to provide. This information is used to respond and troubleshoot. Licence processing is necessary to provide and administer licensed access. Support and related records are retained as needed to address your request, maintain the service and meet applicable legal obligations. Payment records held by Lemon Squeezy follow its retention policy.

Your controls and requests
You control selected media, saved settings and audio reaction through the app. Local settings and logs remain until removed or replaced; logs rotate automatically. Removing local data can reset preferences or affect licence access. Contact support for help locating or removing it, and deactivate first if you want to release a paid activation.

You may contact hello@mablook.com to request access, correction or deletion of personal information held by Mablook. Depending on applicable law, you may also have rights to portability, restriction, objection and to complain to a data-protection authority. Requests concerning information held independently by Microsoft or Lemon Squeezy should be directed to that provider. Keep your licence key private.

Changes
This policy may be updated to reflect changes in HYPNIX or its data practices. The revision date identifies the version presented with the Store listing.
