Lotus - original HYPNIX wallpaper.

This is an original, clean-room HYPNIX implementation. It renders an audio-reactive
blooming lotus using generic, non-copyrightable techniques (a stack of rotated, scaled
petal layers with a time-based breeze, a glowing center, palette tinting, edge
desaturation, a soft vignette and a tanh softening curve).

The visual concept - a layered flower blooming and swaying - is a general idea. No
third-party shader code is used: the petal field, the breeze, the layer accumulation, the
center glow and the audio mapping were all authored by HYPNIX, with our own parameters
(calmer, slower motion). Motion is time-based; the 64 audio bands only modulate the
brightness of the petals by frequency, and the scene stays coherent in silence. Colors
come from the theme palette, so the pink/amber theme yields a warm rose.
