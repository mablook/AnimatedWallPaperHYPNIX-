Spectral Bloom - original HYPNIX wallpaper.

This is an original, clean-room HYPNIX implementation. It renders flowing spectral
silk using generic, non-copyrightable techniques (linear HDR frame feedback with
warp/decay, a separable two-pass bloom and a display-only composite), driven by
the WASAPI audio spectrum.

No third-party shader code is used. All parameters were authored by HYPNIX.
