Visual and technical study references:

- Gargantua 2 by AIandDesign: https://www.shadertoy.com/view/NfVXDd
- NASA / Jeremy Schnittman: https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/13326/

The references were studied for light bending, disk structure and HDR presentation.
The shipped HLSL is independently written; no reference shader, texture or bloom
implementation is bundled. It models a non-rotating Schwarzschild exterior with
midpoint ray integration, a Gaussian emitting layer, periodic plasma noise,
Doppler brightness and per-monitor HDR blur. This is an artistic visualization,
not a complete Kerr-Newman simulation. See docs/EVENT_HORIZON_STUDY.md.
